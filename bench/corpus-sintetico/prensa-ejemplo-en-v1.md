# Press PR-100 technical note — bolt tightening and seal replacement

Synthetic document. The equipment, the codes and the figures are invented.

## 1. Scope

This note complements the Spanish maintenance manual of the example press
PR-100 and covers only the housing cover.

## 2. Tightening torque

The tightening torque for the housing cover bolts is 45 N·m dry. With a
lubricated thread, reduce it to 38 N·m so the housing material is not
overloaded.

WARNING: never tighten the cover bolts in a circular sequence. Uneven
tightening distorts the housing and ruins the axial clearance setting.

## 3. Seal replacement

1. Remove the four cover bolts.
2. Pull the cover straight out without scoring the seal seat.
3. Fit the new lip seal with the spring facing the oil side.
4. Refit the cover and tighten the bolts crosswise.

## 4. Shaft run-out check

Measure shaft run-out with a dial indicator before refitting the cover. The
acceptable total indicator reading is 0.03 mm.
